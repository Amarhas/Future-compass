Future Compass Privacy Policy

Plain-language privacy information for a local-first youth discovery app.

🔒 Local-first👪 Parent-controlled🚫 No ads💳 No card data

Last updated: May 2026

Important: This page is written in plain language so families can understand the app. It is not legal advice.

Plain-English Summary

Future Compass helps kids try small activities, reflect on what they noticed, and build clue badges over time. It is built to be local-first and privacy-conscious.

  • Future Compass does not require a child account.
  • Future Compass does not store child progress in a Future Compass database.
  • Future Compass does not sell child data.
  • Future Compass does not use ads or behavioral tracking.
  • Future Compass does not ask children for full names, addresses, school names, contact information, photos, voice recordings, or precise location.
  • Progress is stored on the device/browser being used.
  • Parents or adults control whether to export, save, delete, or share progress.

Basic rule: Sell to adults. Design for kids. Avoid collecting child data.

What Future Compass Is

Future Compass is a youth discovery tool. It helps children try short activities, reflect on what they noticed, and build clue badges based on practice.

Future Compass is not a diagnosis, assessment, psychological test, school record, therapy service, counseling service, or career assignment system.

A badge is a clue. It is not a permanent label.

What Data the App Stores

The current version stores progress locally in the browser. This may include:

  • profile nickname
  • selected theme
  • activity progress
  • reflection answers
  • badge evidence
  • saved timestamps
  • backup/export file contents if an adult chooses to export them

This information stays on the device/browser unless the user chooses to export or share it. Future Compass cannot see, recover, or access local progress from our side.

What We Do Not Collect

The current hosted app does not directly collect or store:

  • child names
  • child email addresses
  • home addresses
  • school names
  • phone numbers
  • photos
  • voice recordings
  • precise location
  • payment card details
  • child account credentials
  • centralized child profiles
  • cloud progress records

We also do not currently use advertising networks, behavioral advertising, social posting features, public child profiles, analytics pixels, or third-party tracking scripts.

Profile Nicknames

Future Compass may let a parent or user create a local profile nickname. Please do not use a child’s full legal name, school name, address, email, phone number, or other personal details as a nickname.

Good examples include Explorer, Builder, Kid 1, or a simple first-name initial. Avoid full names, school names, addresses, email addresses, and phone numbers.

Reflections and Free-Text Answers

Some activities may include reflection prompts, such as “What part did you like most?” Please do not enter private details in these fields.

Use simple observations like “I liked testing the tower” or “Saving money was harder than spending it.” Avoid full names, school names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, teacher names, private family information, medical information, or location details.

Backups and Exports

Future Compass may allow an adult to export a backup file. That backup may include local progress, profile nicknames, activity answers, reflections, timestamps, and badge evidence.

Only share a backup file with someone you trust. Future Compass does not automatically send backup files to us. If you export a backup, you control where that file goes.

Deleting Data

Because progress is stored locally, you can delete local app data by using the app’s reset option, clearing browser storage/site data, deleting downloaded backup files, or using a different browser/device with no saved progress.

If you clear browser data, local progress may be permanently lost unless you saved a backup.

Grown-Up Gate

Future Compass may include a simple grown-up gate to separate child-facing activities from adult interpretation. This gate is not real authentication, encryption, or a secure account system. It exists only to separate concerns inside the app.

Hosting

Future Compass is currently hosted as a static website using GitHub Pages. The app itself does not run a backend database for child progress. GitHub may process normal website request information according to GitHub’s own privacy practices.

Payments and Paid Products

If Future Compass offers paid products in the future, payment should be handled through established third-party payment or commerce platforms. Future Compass should not collect or store payment card information directly.

Payment providers may collect information needed to process a purchase, such as purchaser name, email address, billing information, payment details, and transaction records. That information is handled by the payment provider or commerce platform according to their own policies.

Future Compass should not ask children to enter payment information.

Children Under 13

Future Compass is designed with children in mind, including children under 13, but the current architecture is designed to avoid collecting child personal information directly.

Parents and guardians should supervise use of the app. Children should not enter private personal information into the app.

Third-Party Links

Future Compass may link to third-party websites or services, such as GitHub, payment providers, download platforms, workshop registration tools, or educational resources. Those services have their own privacy policies.

What We Will Not Do

  • sell child data
  • run behavioral ads aimed at children
  • require child accounts for the core local-first app
  • store child progress in a central database without a clear privacy update and parent-controlled consent path
  • ask children for unnecessary personal details
  • make child progress public
  • use reflections to create hidden behavioral profiles
  • collect payment card information directly

If the Product Changes

If Future Compass later adds accounts, cloud sync, educator dashboards, subscriptions, analytics, or other features that change how data is handled, this privacy policy should be updated before those features are released.

The privacy rule should remain: minimize data collection, keep parents in control, and avoid collecting child data unless it is truly necessary.
