# Project Narrative

Future Compass helps young people explore future-oriented skills through small, low-pressure activities.

The app is not intended to assign a child a career, diagnose personality, or create a fixed identity label. Instead, it helps a child try things, reflect on what happened, and build evidence of practice over time.

## Product promise

Future Compass should help a young person answer:

- What kinds of activities have I tried?
- What did I enjoy or find meaningful?
- What skills am I practicing?
- What should I try next?

## Core loop

```text
Try a small activity
↓
Choose how to start
↓
Reflect
↓
Earn badge evidence
↓
Review patterns with a grown-up
↓
Pick the next small challenge
```

## Design stance

Future Compass should feel playful, colorful, and approachable. It should not feel like a school worksheet, an adult career dashboard, or a personality test.

## Long-term direction

The product may eventually support:

- local child profiles
- learning benchmark paths
- age-band activity tracks
- parent/counselor reports
- PWA installability
- optional app packaging

The immediate priority is durability: profiles, schema versioning, backup/import, accessibility, and clean documentation.
