# Three-Activity Pilot

This phase proves that the focused mission/scaffold pattern works across different activity types, not just a hands-on building challenge.

## Pilot activities

```text
Build a Paper Tower
The $20 Choice
Make Someone's Day Easier
```

## Skill spread

| Activity | Primary pattern | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Build a Paper Tower | Build / test / iterate | Tests hands-on problem solving and design iteration |
| The $20 Choice | Plan / compare / explain | Tests budgeting, tradeoffs, values, and delayed thinking |
| Make Someone's Day Easier | Notice / help / reflect | Tests empathy, initiative, social awareness, and respectful support |

## Product test question

The question is not whether all three activities use the exact same wording.

The question is:

```text
Can the same focused mission flow support physical, financial/planning, and social/helper activities without feeling forced?
```

## Design rules

- Keep the child-facing flow playful and light.
- Keep badges as clues, not identity labels.
- Keep parent/adult research notes plain-language.
- Keep the app local-first and trust-conscious.
- Do not add new base activities during this phase.
- Do not change the core engine unless the renderer needs a small generalization.

## Test checklist

After this PR merges and GitHub Pages redeploys:

1. Open the hosted app.
2. Confirm Paper Tower still works.
3. Move to The $20 Choice and confirm it has a focused mission flow.
4. Move to Make Someone's Day Easier and confirm it has a focused mission flow.
5. Confirm each activity has activity-specific nudges, not Paper Tower wording.
6. Confirm Next / Back / Skip controls work on all three.
7. Confirm Adult view shows grown-up coaching notes for all three.
8. Confirm Badge Center still works.
9. Confirm privacy/terms/trust links and warnings still work.
10. Confirm the experience still feels colorful, mobile-first, and not worksheet-like.

## Phase line

Do not expand the remaining activities until this three-activity pilot passes for tone, clarity, variety, and parent usefulness.
