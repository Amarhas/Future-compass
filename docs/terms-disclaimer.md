# Future Compass Terms and Disclaimer

**Last updated:** May 2026

This page is written in plain language. It is not legal advice.

## Plain-English Summary

Future Compass is a youth discovery tool. It helps kids try small activities, reflect on what they noticed, and build clue badges over time.

By using Future Compass, you understand that it is a supportive discovery tool, not a professional service or official assessment.

## What Future Compass Is

Future Compass is:

- a skill-discovery prototype
- a guided activity system
- a reflection and badge-evidence tool
- a parent/mentor support tool
- a way to notice patterns over time

## What Future Compass Is Not

Future Compass is not:

- a diagnosis
- a psychological test
- a therapy or counseling service
- a school record system
- a formal career assessment
- a guarantee of career, education, or financial outcomes
- a replacement for parents, teachers, counselors, mentors, doctors, or other qualified professionals

## Badges Are Clues, Not Labels

Future Compass badges are evidence of practice. They should not be treated as permanent identity labels.

A badge means:

> I practiced this kind of work, reflected on it, and built evidence over time.

A badge does not mean:

> This is what I am forever.

## Adult Supervision

Future Compass is designed for children and young people, but adults should supervise use of the app and materials.

Adults should help children choose safe activities, avoid sharing private details, and interpret results with care.

## No Guaranteed Results

Future Compass may help children and adults notice patterns, but it does not guarantee any specific academic, career, behavioral, emotional, or financial outcome.

The goal is better exploration and conversation, not guaranteed prediction.

## Local-First Prototype

The current app stores progress locally in the browser/device. If browser storage is cleared, progress may be lost unless a backup was exported.

The app is provided as a prototype and may change over time.

## Grown-Up Gate

The grown-up gate is only a simple separation between child-facing activities and adult interpretation. It is not real authentication, encryption, or security.

## Paid Products

If Future Compass offers paid products in the future, those products may include printable kits, guides, workshops, or licenses.

Payment should be handled through established third-party checkout or commerce platforms. Future Compass should not collect payment card information directly.

Any refund, access, or purchase terms for a paid product should be shown at the point of purchase.

## AI-Assisted Development

Future Compass may be developed with AI-assisted tools under human direction and review. AI tools may help draft code, documentation, or activity ideas, but product direction, privacy decisions, and child-facing releases should be reviewed by a human before release.

Future Compass is not an AI companion, chatbot friend, therapist, counselor, or automated decision-maker for children.

## Changes

Future Compass may change over time. If the app adds accounts, cloud sync, educator dashboards, subscriptions, analytics, payments, or AI product features, the privacy and terms pages should be updated before those features are released.
