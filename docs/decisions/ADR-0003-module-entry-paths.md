# ADR-0003: Use Module Entry Paths

## Status

Accepted

## Decision

Future Compass activities will include a Start Here layer with multiple entry paths:

- Show Me
- Tell Me
- Let Me Try
- Talk Me Through
- Surprise Me

## Reason

Children may not know where to begin when presented with a task. Entry paths reduce friction without assigning fixed learning-style labels.

## Consequences

- Activities become modules, not just step lists.
- Future activity data should include lead-in content.
- The app can support different starting needs without labeling the child.

## Safe to touch

Yes, within the current product rule: entry paths may be renamed, refined, or expanded, but should not become fixed learner labels.
